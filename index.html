Dankort Øremærket

A concept that gives 1–3 øre from every Dankort payment to Danish nature. Tagline: “Kortet det giver tilbage”, the card that gives back.

ResearchConceptUX/UIFront-end
Visit live site ↗
The brief
The agency 1508 A/S challenged us to strengthen Dankort against foreign payment cards and get young Danes (20–45) to choose it.
The idea
Dankort Øremærket: every payment gives 1–3 øre to Danish nature through Den Danske Naturfond. It turns an everyday habit into a small, visible contribution.
My role
I worked across the whole project: research and target-group insights, shaping the Øremærket concept, UX and UI design in Figma, and building parts of the front-end.
Outcome
A campaign website that explains the concept and Dankort's benefits: Danish ownership, lower fees for shops, offline payments and Apple Pay.

Student concept for an exam case, not an official Dankort product.

Client project · Team project

TÓKI House

A website for Connie's product development and packaging consultancy that explains 15 years of expertise as a clear journey from concept to market.

ResearchConceptUX/UI
Visit live site ↗
The challenge
Connie had deep expertise from 15+ years in product development, but no clear digital presence that explained what TÓKI does and how working with her goes.
My role
Research and client meetings to understand Connie's business, concept development for the site's structure and messaging, and UX/UI design in Figma.
The approach
We organised her services as one journey: concept and product development, sourcing, production, packaging, value chain and implementation. A client can see where they are and what comes next.
Outcome
A calm, material-focused website with the message “Your partner from concept to market”, giving TÓKI a clearer digital direction.
